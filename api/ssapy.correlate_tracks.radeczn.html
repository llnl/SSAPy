radeczn

ssapy.correlate_tracks.radeczn(orbit, arc, **kw)[source][source]

Compute right ascension, declination, range, rates, and anomaly wraps.

Parameters:

  • orbit (object or list of objects) – Orbital object(s) containing orbital parameters. Must include meanMotion and t. May include r to determine scalar or array-like orbit handling.

  • arc (structured array) – Observation arc containing time, rStation_GCRF, and vStation_GCRF fields. May include satID to determine scalar or array-like arc handling.

  • kwargs (dict, optional) – Additional keyword arguments passed to ssapy.compute.radec().

Returns:

Tuple containing rr, dd, zz, pmrr, pmdd, dzzdt, and nwrap arrays.

Return type:

tuple
